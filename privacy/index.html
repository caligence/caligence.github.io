Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

Summary

WisprWave processes your voice on your own device. It doesn't send your audio or transcribed text to Caligence. This website uses no cookies or analytics. The rest of this policy gives the details, including the few cases where other services are involved.

Who we are

Caligence Limited ("Caligence", "we") is a company incorporated in Hong Kong. We are responsible for this website and for WisprWave. For any privacy question, email contact@caligence.com.

WisprWave

Audio. WisprWave uses your microphone while you dictate and transcribes the audio on your device. It normally holds audio in memory. In Legacy Mode, it records to a temporary file (recording.wav) in your Mac's temporary folder. That file stays there until your next Legacy Mode recording replaces it or macOS clears it.

Transcribed text. WisprWave pastes text into the app you're using and doesn't keep a transcript history. Once text is in another app, that app's own privacy practices apply.

Clipboard. To paste, WisprWave briefly places the text on your clipboard, then restores your previous contents about half a second later. If you use a clipboard manager, it may record the text during that time.

Permissions. It requests Microphone access to hear you and Accessibility access to send the paste keystroke.

Stored on your device. Downloaded models (on Mac, in Application Support/WisprWave/Models) and your settings, such as your hotkey and chosen mode. We can't access these.

Network use. WisprWave uses the network to download speech models. Mac models come from Hugging Face, and Linux models come from GitHub. Those services receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. The Linux installer also downloads its setup tools. Dictating doesn't require a connection.

No tracking or accounts. WisprWave's own code has no analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and no user accounts. We don't receive your audio, your text or information about how you use the app.

This website

We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising on this site. Two service providers are involved in delivering it:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process visitor IP addresses to deliver and secure it.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's fonts, so your browser connects to Google and shares your IP address.

WisprWave downloads and source code are hosted on GitHub, where GitHub's own policies apply.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you include in your message. We use this to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. Our email is hosted by Proton. We keep correspondence only as long as we need it for those purposes or to meet legal obligations. We don't sell it or use it for marketing without your consent.

Anything you post in a public GitHub issue is public, and GitHub's policies apply.

Your rights

You can ask whether we hold personal data about you, request a copy and ask us to correct or delete it, including under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. Depending on where you live, local law may give you further rights. Email contact@caligence.com to make a request.

Children

Our website and WisprWave aren't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top.
