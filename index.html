Our product

WisprWave

Dictation for the Mac that types into whichever app you are using. Speech recognition runs locally, using Whisper and Parakeet models on your own machine.

How we build

01

Do the work locally

If a task can run on your device, it should. Your data stays with you, and the software keeps working when you are offline.

02

Ask for only what's needed

WisprWave has no sign-up and no tracking in its code. It asks for the two macOS permissions it needs to work, and we explain why.

03

Show the work

WisprWave's code, release history and known limits are public, so you can check what we say about it.
