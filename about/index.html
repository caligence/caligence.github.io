About Caligence

Caligence Limited designs, builds and publishes its own AI-native apps for mobile and desktop platforms.

What we make

Our first product is WisprWave, a dictation app for Mac with a Linux version. It has been in public release since February 2026, with six releases so far.

It reflects what we want from software in general. It solves one clear problem, runs on the device you already own, and doesn't ask you to hand over your data or open an account.

How we work

01

Local first

We run work on your device when it's practical. It's better for privacy, and the software keeps working without a connection.

02

Transparent

We say what a product does and doesn't do, including where data goes, what permissions it needs and what isn't finished yet.

03

Open source

WisprWave's code and release history are public, and each release lists what changed.

What's next

We're continuing to develop WisprWave, and moving Mac releases to Apple Developer ID signing and notarization, so they install without a security warning.

We also have mobile apps in the works, including for Android. We'll list them here when they ship.

Founder

Caligence was founded by Pankaj K, who has 10 years of experience designing, building and shipping large-scale software, previously at Google, Microsoft and Amazon.
